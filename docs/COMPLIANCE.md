# Public repository compliance boundary

This repository is **public research**, not a client-room or consultation workspace.

## Never commit or infer

- Screenshots of expert-network screening, invitations, NDAs, client messages, internal project terms or the identity of an undisclosed consulting client.
- Material non-public information (MNPI), unpublished company performance, confidential ASP or volume discounts, proprietary procurement terms, customer allocations, roadmaps learned under duty of confidence or any private former-employer knowledge.
- Details from conversations with current or former employees in breach of their duties.
- Private identities, access keys, API tokens, personal email or legal/compliance representations.
- Client hypotheses disguised as confirmed facts. Public signals may be analyzed without attributing a confidential client motive.

## Safe inputs

Company IR statements, SEC filings, publicly posted specification pages, broadly accessible public cloud instance lists, vendor launch material labeled as claims, independent published benchmarks (under their licenses), public research with appropriate attribution and explicit hypothetical models.

## Contribution checklist

1. Is the information publicly accessible to an independent reader with the provided link?
2. Can any detail identify a confidential consultation/client by context or timing?
3. Is the source date and vendor/independent nature clear?
4. Is the numerical metric correctly scoped (CPU-only vs CPU+GPU, unit vs revenue share, list vs ASP)?
5. Is this authored analysis based on public sources rather than privileged employment/consultation knowledge?
6. If uncertain, do not merge. Move confidential work to an authorized separate private environment (not this repository).

This is a conservative editorial boundary, not a substitute for individual contractual or securities-law compliance advice.
